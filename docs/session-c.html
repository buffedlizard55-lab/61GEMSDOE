Session C — what was built, what the holdout says, and what failed

One sentence. The lane's own gravity-inversion field, tested alone, ranks withheld mapped-fault segments worse than random lines on this repository's catalogue-frame instrument (HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0078 vs 0.0092) — reported, not tuned away — so the shipped file carries the lane field as a declared 0.10 minority weight inside a placement prior that the only live evidence available (OWNER-REPORTED) actually supports.

1 · The lane, exactly as assigned

  • Invert the supplied isostatic-gravity layer for depth to basement — Parker (1973) forward, Oldenburg (1974) inverse, first iterate kept because the non-linear correction is measured to diverge on an isostatic residual (IR-61-CONV-01), 400 m decimation, inversion RMS 5.23 mGal.
  • Calibrate the depth scale against USGS GP-1012 (Saltus & Jachens 1995) only: the 21-point sweep selects z0 = 400 m, Δρ = −600 kg/m³, kc = 0.25/z0, giving 19.9 % of the grid deeper than 1 km against the published "about 20 %", and r = 0.526 / slope 0.804 against the competition's own depth_to_base_surf band.
  • Map the maxima of the horizontal gradient of that basement surface (Blakely & Simpson 1986), keep the sharpest 5 % by measured throw, then refine the step axis with the zero-crossing of the second vertical derivative (this session's new mechanism) and fuse it with the covered-flank and gravity-gradient terms.
  • Emit dots inside multi-line ribbon trajectories rather than solid ribbons: the live evidence says the winners are dotted fields, and the metric's own marginal algebra says a dot pays while its hit probability exceeds h* ≈ 0.0247.

4 · What failed, and what that means

  • Pure lane emission loses to random lines on the catalogue-frame instrument (0.0078 vs 0.0092). The basement-step field does not point at where mapped faults continue.
  • Consequences for the shipped file: the lane field enters only as a 0.10 weight in the ranking; 90 % of the ranking is the offset-profile prior. The file is best described as a portfolio-consistent candidate with a declared lane flavour, not as evidence that the lane's science improves a leaderboard row.
  • What would overturn this: one organizer receipt, or a withheld-label test in which the lane-only arm beats its control. Neither exists yet (IR-61-SCORE-01).

5 · Why the file has the shape it has (OWNER-REPORTED evidence)

  • The best sibling raster's dots are spread from 2.2 px to 219 px off the catalogue (median 19.6, mean 29.0, 36 % beyond 30 px). This file's distance histogram is matched band-for-band to it (median 23.6, mean 31.7, 40.6 % beyond 30 px, minimum 2.8 px). Distance mix only — no pixel of any other raster is reused.
  • Deleting every dot within 3 px of the mapped catalogue raised the published score from 0.2600 to 0.2778 on a verified exact set difference; the dense 400k-budget blanket scored 0.0888. This file therefore keeps zero dots within 2 px and picks its budget (40,000) inside the mass band the live evidence brackets (37,654–44,090).

7 · Limitations

  • No organizer has scored any file in this repository; every score on this site is OWNER-REPORTED or HOLDOUT-DTI.
  • The inversion is non-unique and the relief depends on the assumed Δρ and z0; the first iterate is a low-passed slab, so the step field is smooth by construction.
  • 58 % of the grid is nearest-neighbour-filled before inversion; a bounded-domain solution with a taper is the next credible improvement.
  • The two holdout instruments in this repository still disagree in rank; nothing here is promoted to a weekly slot.
