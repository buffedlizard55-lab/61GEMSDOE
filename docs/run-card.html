Hypothesis

Buried normal fault is a step in the basement surface inverted with depth-dependent fill density (Hildenbrand 1999: 1900/2100/2300/2450 kg/m3, basement 2670); the step is located at HGM maxima and discriminated from intrabasement density contacts by Euler SI=0 source depth consistency and magnetic edge demotion.

Mechanism

Isostatic residual band 13 -> 200m decimation -> inpaint -> 15km regional removal -> Butterworth low-pass kc=0.25/(2*500m) -> per-pixel variable-density Bouguer solve t=dg/(2πG*avgΔρ(t)) -> upsample 100m -> Blakely-Simpson HGM maxima throw>=80m -> corridor 1km -> Euler depth from bands 18/11 -> mag edge from band3 -> logistic habitat over 30 features (19 bands + var-depth + HGM + throw + corridor + basin + Euler + grad + mag_edge + Euler_cons) OOF per quadrant -> dots 3px tile 3px prune.

Named non-fault process that could mimic it

Intrabasement lithologic contact (granitic/mafic) with Δρ~200 kg/m3 over 2 km gives same gravity step as 1000m basement offset at 400 kg/m3; also caldera fill, hydrothermal alteration, paleo-weathered horizon. Discriminated by Euler depth (basement edge shallow ~ inverted depth, intrabasement deep) and by magnetic edge coincidence (intrabasement contact often magnetic, basin step non-magnetic).
