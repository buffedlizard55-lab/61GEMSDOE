Hypothesis

the surface fault trace of a normal fault lies z_b*cot(dip) toward the footwall from the horizontal-gradient maximum of the inverted basement depth, so projecting the step by that distance places the emitted dots on the trace the metric scores

Mechanism

planar normal fault: the basement step sits at x = z_b*cot(dip) from the surface trace; z_b from the Parker/Oldenburg inversion, dip from the Basin-and-Range literature

Named non-fault process that could mimic it

density contrasts within the basement (igneous contacts, pluton margins, buried volcanic centres) produce an identical horizontal-gradient maximum with no surface scarp; measured with the independent lidar scarp conjunction, which is scored as its own arm
