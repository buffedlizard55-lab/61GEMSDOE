Verdict: negative

The candidate does not beat a budget- and domain-matched random-line null at the pre-registered 95 % level (paired difference 0.00969562979613393, 95 % CI [-0.001902295456289475, 0.021709448195985196]), so the pre-registered promotion rule is not satisfied. The point estimate is positive inside the buried (basin-interior) domain and negative or tied over the full footprint. The single largest term is the irreducible one: the measured positional resolution is 900 m at the median against a 300 m scoring kernel.

Hypothesis

A buried range-front or intrabasin normal fault offsets the pre-Tertiary basement surface under alluvial cover; that offset is a step in the basement depth recovered by inverting the isostatic residual gravity, and it is located at the maximum of the horizontal gradient of the inverted depth.

Mechanism

Parker (1973) Fourier forward operator and Oldenburg (1974) iterative inversion of band 13 iso_grav_anom at drho=400 kg/m3, z0=3000 m, cutoff 5000 m; Canny non-maximum suppression along the gradient; throw measured across an 800 m aperture; emission as multi-line ribbons thinned to isolated dots.

Named non-fault process that could mimic it

A lateral density contrast WITHIN basement: a granitic/mafic contact, a hydrothermally altered zone, a caldera fill or a palaeo-weathered horizon. 200 kg/m3 over a 2 km section is indistinguishable from a 1000 m step of the basement surface at a 400 kg/m3 contrast, and it cuts no fault at all.
