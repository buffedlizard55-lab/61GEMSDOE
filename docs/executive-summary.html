Why this file cannot trigger “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”

That portal error has two known causes and both are engineered out. The writer never clips, fills or repairs: it validates in memory, writes, then re-reads the bytes from disk and aborts unless every one of the 12,279,160 cells is finite and inside [0, 1].

  1. The feature stack's float32 sentinel (-3.4028234663852886e+38) is stripped at cache time and re-checked as no_float32_sentinel.
  2. A footprint mask derived from a feature band can leave scored pixels NaN. The footprint here is taken from the pinned sample_submission.tif itself (5,167,373 finite cells), and cells outside it are written as explicit zeros, not NaN — so the whole raster is finite.
