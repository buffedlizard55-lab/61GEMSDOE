What the buried-structure work established

The isostatic-gravity inversion was calibrated to the USGS GP-1012 statewide depth statistic (fraction deeper than 1 km: 0.203 versus the ≈0.20 anchor); band 15 was only an independent cross-check (Pearson r=0.738). Intrabasement density contrasts remain a live competing explanation: the same gravity edge can arise from a lithologic contact without a fault. H61-J (source-depth consistency) remains an untested hypothesis.
