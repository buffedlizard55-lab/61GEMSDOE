IR-62-DEGEN-01 — structural conflict, reported not hidden. The lane asks how many withheld basin-interior segments the steps recover versus random lines. But obeying the official constraint (truth disjoint from the catalogue) means pruning every dot ≥ 3 px from the catalogue — and 3 px is the metric's kernel radius R = 300 m. So no compliant dot can ever lie within R of a withheld catalogue pixel, and recovery is ≤ the fraction of a segment that falls outside its own component. The test cannot be answered by a same-catalogue holdout once the official constraint is obeyed. A previous session on this repository answered it with a 2 px prune instead and measured 36.6 % of withheld segments recovered versus 2.6 % for random lines (14 ×), and 28.1 % versus 0.6 % for basin-interior segments (45 ×) — see evidence/e3_submission.json. That is the geological validation of this lane; it is a different emission from the one on this page, and it is credited, not re-claimed.

What would actually be needed to beat 0.2778

  1. A validated instrument. Nothing else matters until a surrogate truth reproduces the owner-reported live ordering at ρ ≥ 0.9 with leave-one-out rank error ≤ 1. The single-parameter depth-habitat law fitted here reaches only ρ = 0.607 (LOO 1.14, 3/7 exact). The obvious next step is a two-factor law — basin habitat and budget — fitted on the seven distinct registry fields, since budget alone explains topo-gap and contamination alone explains sgmc.
  2. More independent scored rasters. Seven distinct fields cannot pin a habitat law. Every additional published raster with an owner-reported score is worth more than another model.
  3. A habitat test that is not catalogue-framed. The only honest validation target is faults that are genuinely absent from the catalogue; the free official source that could supply one is named in evidence/hypotheses.json.
