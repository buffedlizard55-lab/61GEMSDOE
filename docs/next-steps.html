Limitations

  • HOLDOUT-DTI is measured against withheld catalogue segments, while the live truth is uncatalogued faults. The evaluator orders unpruned group files like the leaderboard, but its absolute values are not live scores and it cannot see the value of pruning near the catalogue.
  • The GP-1012 calibration uses one published area statistic (fraction of basin area deeper than 1 km), not the digital GP-1012 grids, which are not reachable from this sandbox.
  • Only 8 fold cells (two seeds × four quadrants); CIs are bootstrap over cells and are therefore wide for small effects.
  • The competition data were obtained from a hash-pinned public bridge, not directly from DrivenData (sandbox allow-list); the pins match the organiser's file sizes and grid.
  • Experiment budget exhausted (3/3); no hypothesis outside the lane was run.
