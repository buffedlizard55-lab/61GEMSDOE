Limitations

  • H61-G's HOLDOUT-DTI uses gems61.holdout_segmented v2 (whole segments <=12 px, 4 eroded quadrants, seeds 20+21, mode all) and withholds mapped catalogue segments. It is an internal index, not the competition's hidden uncatalogued-fault score.
  • The shared gems61-pooled-hide-v2 benchmark is a different instrument/fold set. Its 0.064939 value is an internal same-version comparator, not a live projection or score.
  • GP-1012 calibration matches a regional depth statistic (fraction deeper than 1 km), not independent pixelwise truth; its broad 1:2.5M scale cannot identify the cause of individual gravity edges.
  • Intrabasement lithologic density contrasts were not modeled or independently checked; a gravity step is not by itself evidence of a fault.
  • H33-2-B2's 0.2778 association is owner-reported and not tied to an organizer receipt. The official leaderboard observation is account-level only.
  • The current registry audit sees 27 accessible rasters, not the full organizer-side registry. H61-G fails even this partial gate, so the lack of full coverage cannot rescue it.
  • 1 m USGS 3DEP product access and licensing are documented as public/free, but exact project tile availability for this footprint was not checked. It remains conditional.
  • Competition rasters were obtained from a hash-pinned public mirror because direct competition hosts are blocked in the sandbox; this is not organizer provenance.
