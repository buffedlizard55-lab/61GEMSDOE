Limitations and what is still blocking progress

Honest limits of this lane as implemented

  • The inversion agrees only weakly with the provided depth band. Pearson r ≈ −0.18 over the full footprint against band 15, versus −0.77 on a single well-covered 900 x 900 crop. The full-grid number is the honest one. Band 13 is an isostatic residual; the long wavelengths that carry most of the basin-depth signal have already been removed from it, and 57.93% of the grid is outside the survey, so the FFT operates on a filled hole. A bounded-domain inversion (e.g. only the largest valid connected region, with a taper) is the next thing to try.
  • No single band separates the catalogue. Every one of the seven candidate fields has AUC between 0.45 and 0.53 for catalogue membership. Whatever model wins this competition is not a per-pixel threshold on one geophysical band.
  • The withheld set is unknown, so every score here is a proxy. The holdout hides segments of the visible catalogue. The real Phase 1 set is a different, expert-compiled dataset. The two share the disjointness property, which is why the off-catalogue step transfers, but no holdout number in this repository is a leaderboard prediction and none is presented as one.
  • Gravity cannot see the target at full strength. A few hundred metres of resolution is the published limit; a fault with a small vertical throw is simply not in the band.

Blockers that are not technical

  • DrivenData login. The official data tab and the submission page both require an account. This repository cannot download the data or submit a file unattended. Data arrives through a public hash-pinned mirror of the same bytes; the upload is a manual step by the account holder.
  • Egress. The development sandbox reaches github.com, pypi and nothing else: no Dropbox, no USGS, no drivendata.org. Every non-GitHub source in Method is cited but was read through a mirror or a citation, and is labelled that way.
  • No organizer receipts. No score in this repository has been confirmed by the submission page for our bytes. Until one is, every claim of quality here is a holdout measurement.

The competing explanation that must not be forgotten

Density contrasts within basement produce horizontal gravity gradients that look exactly like a fault step. A Jurassic intrusion margin, a basement lithologic contact, or a Tertiary volcanic centre all put a ridge in the horizontal gradient magnitude that no fault explains. This lane cannot separate them, and any ribbon it emits on such a contact is a false positive the metric will charge at alpha = 0.2. Any future scoring of this lane must report that contamination explicitly.
