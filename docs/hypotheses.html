USGS Geophysical Investigations Map GP‑1012 (Saltus & Jachens, 1995) is the prescribed depth-scale reference. It is a folded paper map (USGS Store product 23063) and its host is outside this sandbox's egress allowance (github.com, api.github.com, codeload.github.com, registry.npmjs.org, pypi.org, files.pythonhosted.org only). It is named here as the source a future session must obtain; the band-15 substitution used instead is recorded as an irregularity.
