H61-Q Geodetic strain-rate locator: bands 4 (second invariant), 7 (shear rate), 8 (dilatation rate)Bands 4, 7 and 8 of the supplied stack: band04 geod_2ndinv (geodetic second invariant, the strain-rate tensor magnitude), band07 geod_shearrate (shear rate, angular deformation from GPS/InSAR), band08 geod_dilaterate (volumetric strain). These measure present-day deformation rather than a re-expression of topography or gravity.A fault that is accumulating slip has a geodetic strain-rate maximum across it; the second invariant localises the deformation and the dilatation rate separates opening (normal-fault) from closing regimes.An actively slipping fault that has not produced a mappable scarp is exactly the class the catalogue misses, and geodesy sees it through the regolith. The measured logistic weights are the third and seventh largest of all 28 channels (band07 +0.889, band04 -0.628), so the supplied stack already carries the signal and no session has used it as a primary locator.Every previous locator in this repository is topographic, magnetic or gravity-derived. No session has built a candidate field from the geodetic bands, and the sign of band04 (negative) means the naive 'high strain = fault' reading is wrong and needs the tensor decomposition.Moderate. Blocked canary AUC for a single geodetic band is untested; the model weight says the information is there. PROJECTION ONLY.Low - all inputs are already cached. One holdout run.
H61-R Seismicity-nucleation locator: bands 10 (distance to earthquake) and 16 (earthquake intensity)Bands 10 and 16 of the supplied stack: band10 deq_n100a15 (distance to the nearest earthquake within a 100 km radius, 15-degree azimuth parameterisation) and band16 ieq_n100a15 (earthquake intensity or density, same parameterisation).Microseismicity nucleates on the faults that are critically stressed and weak. A cluster of small events with no mapped trace is a direct indication of an unmapped, currently active fault.This is the only supplied layer that measures present-day fault slip rather than a geological or geophysical proxy for it, so it is the closest available analogue to 'a fault exists here now'. band16 carries the second-largest logistic weight of all 28 channels (+0.890, behind only band19 detrended-elevation slope at +1.343).No session in this repository has used the seismicity bands as a primary locator; they have only ever entered as two of nineteen channels.Moderate. What this session ruled out

  • A hard basement-step corridor as the candidate gate. 0.0364–0.0409 gated vs 0.0606 ungated at the same budget; the corridor indicator's canary AUC against withheld faults is 0.510.
  • Gradient boosting on the same 28 features. 0.1599 on the non-blocked holdout, but blocked AUC 0.518–0.618 and LOQO-DTI 0.0140–0.0718: adjacency, not skill (IR-61-LEAK-01).
  • Soft step / throw priors on the logistic model. 0.0489–0.0522 vs 0.0606 with no prior.
  • 3 × 3 ribbons instead of dots. 0.0184 vs 0.0409; 256,096 emitted px vs 39,794.
