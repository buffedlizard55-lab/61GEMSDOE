#1 Dip-projected basement-step trace, with an independent lidar scarp conjunctionBand 13 of the supplied stack (isostatic gravity anomaly) through the Parker/Oldenburg inversion -> depth to basement; the auxiliary lidar scarp feature raster (topographic-scarp detector) as the competing-explanation test; band 19 (detrended-elevation slope) only in the control arm.Directional geometry: the horizontal-gradient maximum of the inverted basement depth sits z_b*cot(dip) basinward of the surface fault trace. Projecting the step toward its footwall by that distance is a depth-dependent, sign-definite correction, not a symmetric dilation. Where a gravity model and a control-point compilation disagree by hundreds of metres over kilometres, one of them is missing a structure.A regional compilation is interpolated between sparse control; it smooths through small basins, relay ramps and step-overs, so a real fault-bounded sub-basin can be absent from it.
