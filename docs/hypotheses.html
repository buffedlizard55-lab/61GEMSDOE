Rank 1 · H61U-H1 — signed flank-offset persistence

Layers
Band 13 only: isostatic gravity; derived, uncalibrated basement depth.
Physical signature
At each depth-HGM maximum, sample signed basement-depth differences across the local normal at 2 km and 4 km. Require same polarity and min(|Δ2|,|Δ4|)/(max(|Δ2|,|Δ4|)+ε) ≥0.60; emit 3-pixel multi-line ribbons, budget 40,000.
Why absent from catalogue
A concealed basin-interior fault can offset basement without a mapped surface scarp; persistent one-sided depth displacement can survive under cover.
Named non-fault mimic
Finite intrabasement mafic/granitic body, density sheet, or intrusive contact; paired edges may imitate a step.
Different from existing methods
Not simply an HGM/throw cutoff, multi-scale step persistence, or ordinary ribbon. Unlike sibling GEMSDOE52 R2's signed gravity-gradient versus organizer band-15 cover-gradient anti-alignment at 1/3/8 pixels, H1 uses band-13-only inverted depth and same-polarity 2/4 km flank offsets. No band 15 or learned cross-layer feature.
Expected gain/cost rank
Expected DTI gain rank 1/3; medium potential, unquantified. Low implementation cost.
Obtainability
Band 13 is locally cached from a pinned public bridge copy (not a fresh authenticated organizer download). No additional external layer is needed to test scale-invariant geometry; full GP-1012 remains necessary for any absolute-depth calibration and is not local.
Observed result
HOLDOUT-DTI · gems61-pooled-hide-v2 · 12,074 withheld positives: 0.041351 (95% CI 0.035332–0.048100). Paired H1−HGM difference −0.003009 (95% CI −0.010105–0.004951). Final-dot uniqueness failed; no artifact emitted.

Rank 2 · H61U-H2 — along-strike throw taper at concealed tips

Layers
Band 13 gravity and its derived inverted basement depth only.
Physical signature
For connected depth-HGM step segments at least 20 km long, sample signed 2 km normal throw every 400 m along strike. Require one polarity through the central 60% and a monotone or single-hump envelope whose terminal-20% median absolute throw is ≤0.20 of segment maximum. Do not use catalogue traces to define or orient segments.
Why absent from catalogue
A buried fault may taper to subsurface tips under basin fill without surface rupture or scarp; the buried throw envelope can remain observable.
Named non-fault mimic
A finite intrabasement density body can also produce edges that taper near its ends; taper alone does not prove faulting.
Different from existing methods
Tests along-strike envelope of signed basement displacement, rather than local throw threshold, HGM persistence, or trace continuity. It does not use band 15 or catalogue geometry.
Expected gain/cost rank
Expected DTI gain rank 2/3; low-to-medium potential, uncertain. Medium implementation cost.
Obtainability
Uses cached band 13 and the competition grid already in the repository. GP-1012 remains separate calibration-only data; no new official feature is required.
Status
Prospective, untested, and not authorized as a fallback for H1.

Rank 3 · H61U-H3 — compare gravity source classes

Layers
Band 13 gravity and derived inverted basement-depth candidate normals only.
Physical signature
Fit each fixed 1-D gravity profile with two preregistered forward models: basement-interface step versus finite buried density sheet/contact. Retain a step only if the step model beats the density model by ΔBIC ≥10, with nuisance ranges frozen before holdout scoring.
Why absent from catalogue
A fault beneath basin fill may have no mapped surface expression; the gravity profile can retain a source-shape signature.
Named non-fault mimic
Finite-width mafic/granitic intrusion or lithologic contact within basement; the competing density-sheet model is designed to reject this cause.
Different from existing methods
Existing H61 methods detect or corroborate edges; H3 would compare raw gravity profile residuals under two physical source models. No band 15 or catalogue-derived feature.
Expected gain/cost rank
Expected DTI gain rank 3/3; uncertain/low potential because gravity source non-uniqueness may dominate. High implementation cost.
Obtainability
Requires only local band 13 for the uncalibrated test. Full GP-1012 is still needed for depth-scale calibration; official grid is identified but unavailable in full.
Status
Prospective, untested, and not authorized as a fallback for H1.

Prior-art and stop discipline

Known local prior art includes HGM maxima, throw thresholds, regular ribbons, gravity–magnetic conjunctions, Euler depth, corridor/LiDAR features, and H61G's 0/2/5-pixel multi-scale step persistence. GEMSDOE52 R2 uses band-13/band-15 signed normal anti-alignment and cross-view interactions. The bounded novelty claim is only that H1's exact signed 2/4 km flank-offset test was not found in the reviewed local implementation—not a claim of global priority. The former H4 idea was withdrawn after discovering direct overlap with H61G.

The frozen registration permits only H1 in this run. H2/H3 cannot be substituted after observing H1's result; any future test requires a new preregistration. See machine-readable ranked hypotheses, frozen registration, and final outcome.

No experiment was authorized to use organizer band 15, a leaderboard artifact, or previous submission raster as a predictor.
