Buried-structure method and limitations

Lane definition

Use the supplied isostatic gravity to estimate basement relief; calibrate only the depth scale against USGS Saltus & Jachens GP-1012; identify horizontal-gradient maxima in the basement-depth surface as candidate buried steps; emit ribbons; compare basin-interior withheld-segment recovery against random lines; treat intrabasement density contrasts as the named non-fault mimic.

What the current H61-G implementation actually does

Band 13 is regional-separated at 15 km, decimated to 400 m, and converted by a low-passed Bouguer-slab first iterate with z0=500 m, Δρ=-300 kg/m³, and kc_scale=Butterworth order 4.0, kc = 5.000e-04 rad/m (kc_scale=0.5). GP-1012 scale anchors set a >1 km fraction of 0.203 (target ≈0.20); band 15 is used as an independent check only (Pearson r=0.738). The current production surface is the first low-passed slab iterate, not a demonstrated converged nonlinear Parker–Oldenburg inversion.

The six-step nonlinear test in evidence/measure.json ends with 130.546 m maximum change at iteration 6 (1 m tolerance) and a 1.4038 mGal residual. The residual falls over the recorded steps; this does not prove general divergence, but it also does not establish convergence. Treat basement relief and any step maps as provisional. This method/provenance mismatch is flagged as IR-61-CONV-02.

Steps and H61-G locator

At 400 m the existing run found 8,999 HGM maxima, 7,527 after a ≥80 m throw filter, and 4,104 persistent maxima. The historical H61-G candidate used those basement steps as a ±1 km corridor and band-19 detrended-elevation slope ridges as the 100 m locator. Its file is dots, not the requested basement-step ribbons; this is recorded as a lane/emission mismatch.

H61-L top hypothesis (stopped)

H61-L uses an upstream public 12-band terrain-descriptor raster derived from USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM tiles (not raw lidar and not a fault prediction). It combines shortwave slope excess, 10 m/50 m slope break, crest/base curvature and structure-tensor coherence, then forms 3-pixel scarp ribbons inside basement-step corridors under fill. The descriptor covers 3730×3292 cells and 75.4% of the valid competition footprint; SHA-256 d580bb8bdcdb941e32fefb8b38044bc5bf04e199bf2e83498c3576e6fc465568. The current-run surface failed the strict pre-placement gate, so that run did not reach holdout. A different main-branch H61-L variant has a historical HOLDOUT-DTI result but its final TIFF fails the strict post-placement gate and is not cleared.
