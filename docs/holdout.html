Holdout evidence

Protocol (parallel-run rule 2): withhold whole fault segments (≤ 12 px chunks between intersections) inside four spatially eroded quadrants, with two independent draws; derive every catalogue-based quantity only from the visible faults; mask visible fault pixels pixel-exactly; score pooled DTI with α = 0.2, β = 0.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel. Withheld positives: 22,641 px over 8 cells.
