Interpretation: H61U-H1 recovers more withheld basin-interior segments than random lines, but fewer than unfiltered basement-depth HGM maxima. Its pooled DTI point estimate is also lower than the HGM control. The final-dot uniqueness gate failed, so no file was emitted.

Frozen holdout design

  • Evaluator: gems61-pooled-hide-v2.
  • Hide 20% of complete 8-connected catalogue components with a 3-pixel context buffer; four spatial quadrants; fold seed 20261011. Whole segments, not individual pixels, are withheld.
  • Mask visible catalogue pixels exactly in the scored domain. H61U-H1 itself uses no catalogue features and no band-15 information.
  • Pooled DTI: alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel.
  • Paired percentile bootstrap over 161 globally aligned 20 km blocks, 1,000 draws, seed 520810. Intervals are conditional on the fixed folds and catalogue truth—not a leaderboard interval.
  • Compare H1 to unfiltered HGM maxima and matched random lines at the same 40,000-pixel budget and footprint.

Outcome and limitations

The final uniqueness scan covered 748 accessible aligned sibling-repository rasters (not this repository's own historical TIFFs) and stopped before TIF creation because final-dot overlap exceeded the 70% limit. The holdout does not justify submitting the method: H1 did not beat its unfiltered HGM control and no distinct artifact is available. Detailed receipts are in the full run card and the compact outcome JSON.

Public official leaderboard rows are account-level and not comparable to this local DTI. GP-1012 scale calibration remains blocked; organizer band 15 was not used.
