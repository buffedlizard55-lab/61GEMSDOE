Instrument scope: the evaluator measures recovery of withheld mapped catalogue components. It is not the organizer's private new-fault truth or a public leaderboard score.

Fold construction and scoring

  • Hide 20% of whole 8-connected fault components; do not split segments into independent pixels.
  • Use 3-pixel context buffer, four spatial quadrants, seed 20261011.
  • Candidate features are label-free in H61U. Any catalogue-derived feature in another registered use must be built from visible faults only; visible catalogue pixels are pixel-exactly masked in scored terms.
  • Pooled DTI: alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel.
  • Paired block bootstrap: globally aligned 20 km blocks, 1,000 draws, seed 520810; 95% interval is conditional on fixed folds and proxy truth.
  • Controls: same-budget unfiltered basement-depth HGM maxima and matched random lines.

Run-specific counts and results

HOLDOUT-DTI · evaluator gems61-pooled-hide-v2 · 12,074 withheld positives: H61U-H1 0.041351 (95% CI 0.035332–0.048100), HGM 0.044359 (0.036847–0.051764), random 0.016030 (0.010328–0.021541). See full holdout report.

HOLDOUT-SEGMENT-RECOVERY · evaluator gems61-pooled-hide-v2 · 12,074 withheld positives: 128/318 H1 segments recovered within 300 m; HGM 138/318; random 40/318.

Leakage policy

Each registered candidate feature is scored alone as a leakage canary. AUC >0.90 is leakage-suspect until explained and is a promotion blocker. H61U's maximum was 0.555204; this only checks the registered features and protocol.

Interpretation

H61U-H1 was better than random lines but not better than unfiltered HGM maxima under the paired comparison, and its final-dot uniqueness test failed. No submission file was written. The local negative result must not be converted to an organizer score or used to justify post-hoc tuning.

The evaluator is shared tooling, not a private fork; see `src/gems61/evaluate_holdout.py` and the run receipt.
