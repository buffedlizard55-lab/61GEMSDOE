What this lane tests

Alluvial cover is one reason fault catalogues miss faults. A buried range‑front or intrabasin normal fault offsets the basement surface even where it leaves no scarp, and that offset is visible in the isostatic gravity field. This lane inverts the provided isostatic‑gravity layer to basement relief with the Parker (1973) / Oldenburg (1974) iteration, locates the horizontal‑gradient maxima of the recovered basement depth, measures the throw of each step in metres, and emits the result as multi‑line ribbons of isolated dots — because gravity locates such a step only to a few hundred metres at best.
