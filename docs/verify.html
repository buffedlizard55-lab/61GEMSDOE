Irregularities carried forward

  • IR-57-INSTR-01 — the catalogue-frame holdout ranks placements by coverage of a large population and has measured ρ = −0.93 against 15 owner-reported live scores. It is used for structural fidelity only.
  • IR-61-SIGN-01 — the first build of the inversion had a stray leading minus, giving corr(depth, gravity) = +0.63 where the supplied band gives −0.685. Caught by requiring the sign, not by inspecting a picture; fixed and re-run.
  • IR-61-UNIQ-01 — dot-overlap against a dense sibling surface is degenerate (2.7 M positive cells covers half the footprint), so the uniqueness gate uses Jaccard when the reference is dense and the one-sided fraction only when it is sparse.
